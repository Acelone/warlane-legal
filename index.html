Privacy Policy

War Lane: Roguelike · DNAce Studio · Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy explains what data the mobile game War Lane: Roguelike ("the Game") processes, why, and the choices you have. The Game is developed and published by DNAce Studio ("we", "us"), Indonesia.

Summary

  • No account and no sign-up.
  • Game progress is stored on your device, not on our servers.
  • Ads are provided by Unity LevelPlay (IronSource) and its ad networks.
  • Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never receive your card details.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Data processed

1. Advertising data

When ads are enabled, Unity LevelPlay and the ad networks configured in it may process:

  • Advertising ID (Android AD_ID) and similar device identifiers
  • IP address, which may indicate an approximate location
  • Device model, operating system version, language, and app version
  • Ad events such as impressions, clicks, and completion of a rewarded ad

This data is used to serve rewarded and interstitial ads, measure ad performance, and prevent ad fraud. Personalized ads are used only if you allow them. You choose at first launch and can change the choice any time in Settings > Privacy.

2. Purchase data

Google Play processes payments. We do not receive your name, card number, or billing address. The Game receives the product ID and transaction ID so it can grant the purchased item and reject duplicate grants. Transaction IDs are stored in the Game's local save to prevent the same purchase being applied twice.

3. Data stored on your device

  • Coins, upgrades, unlocked forms, and entitlements (Remove Ads, Starter Pack, Supporter Pack)
  • Your ad consent choice and ad frequency counters
  • Processed purchase transaction IDs

This data stays in the Game's private storage on your device. It is not uploaded to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its app data.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or precise location. The Game has no account system and no cloud save.

Sharing

  • Unity Technologies / IronSource (LevelPlay) - ad mediation and measurement. See the Unity game player privacy policy.
  • Ad networks configured in LevelPlay - they receive the ad request data described above to serve and measure ads.
  • Google Play and Google Payment - payment processing and purchase verification. See the Google privacy policy.

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose data if required by law or to protect legal rights.

Legal bases (EEA and UK)

  • Consent for personalized advertising. You can withdraw it in Settings > Privacy.
  • Legitimate interests for non-personalized advertising, fraud prevention, and operating the Game.
  • Contract for processing purchases you make.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of your data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. California residents have rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know, delete, and correct; we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising outside the consent you give. Indonesian users have rights under Law No. 27/2022 on Personal Data Protection.

Contact dnacedeveloper@gmail.com. We aim to respond within 30 days. You can also reset your advertising ID in Android settings, and remove local data by uninstalling the Game or clearing its app data.

Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA and UK). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Security and retention

Data sent by the Game and its partners uses HTTPS. Local data is protected by the app sandbox on your device. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Local data is kept until you uninstall the Game or clear its data. Ad partners keep data according to their own policies.

International transfers

Our partners may process data outside Indonesia, including in the EEA and the United States, using standard contractual clauses or other lawful transfer mechanisms.

Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always on this page with the date above. Material changes will be announced in the Game or on this page before they take effect.
